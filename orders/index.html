    Pending Orders¶

    The Orders tab lists every pending order in the book: entry orders waiting for their price, and orders waiting for your confirmation. The badge on the tab counts all pending orders, and it updates live.

    Watch how: Handle pending orders (1:22)

    Columns¶

    Column Meaning
    Order, Account, Instrument, B/S, Lot, Rate The order itself.
    Proximity Distance from the order rate to the market, in pips. It updates live. Negative means the market has crossed the order rate.
    T Order type: ES entry stop, EL entry limit, S stop, L limit, I initiate (market), C close. Hover the header for the full list.
    Time When the order was placed.
    Trader The trader login.
    TR Trader range.

    More columns (Ticket, OCO, trailing stop, Predef.Stop, Predef.Limit, comment and others) can be turned on: right-click a column header and choose Customize columns….

    Orders waiting for you¶

    Watch how: Confirm a client order (0:39)

    When an account's negotiation needs the dealer (see Account Settings), its orders wait here, highlighted at the top of the list. Type I is a market order to open a position. Proximity shows how far the market has moved from the client's price.

    Select the order and press Accept (or right-click → Accept order). Confirm Order shows the order; you can fill it at another rate or for a different amount, or leave them as they are, and press Accept. The order becomes the client's position and leaves the list.

    Quick Confirm (Shift+F3), Quick Execute (Shift+F4) and Quick Reject (Shift+F8) act on the selected order without the window.

    What Reject does on your server

    In ActTrader a dealer reject sends the order back to the client at a price (a re-quote); the quick reject uses the current market price. On the test server, rejecting a waiting market order still opened the client's position at the order's own price. Confirm with your back office how your server handles a rejected order before relying on it.

    Changing an order¶

    Right-click an order:

    • Modify Order (F8) changes its rate or size. It is available for stop, limit and entry orders that have not triggered yet.
    • Stop, Limit and Trailing Stop (entry orders only) attach a stop-loss or take-profit to the entry order. They apply once the order fills; the entry rate does not change. The window works like the one for an open position, measured from the order's rate. The Predef.Stop / Predef.Limit columns show them.
    • Remove order cancels the order for the client after you confirm; the confirmation names the order. Afterwards no order is selected.
    • Deactivate Order (also the Deactivate button) asks the server to deactivate the order, after you confirm.
    • Link OCO Order… links two entry orders as One-Cancels-Other: when one fills, the other is cancelled. Only orders of the same client, instrument and side, one limit and one stop, not yet triggered, can be linked. The OCO column (turn it on with Customize columns…) shows each order's partner.
    • Order Depth shows the instrument's pending orders by price.
    Watch how: Link two orders as OCO (0:45)

    Views¶

    The right-click menu also switches the view: Show Entry, Show Imminent (orders close to triggering) and Show Urgent (orders that need you now).
